# Discovery UX: user stories

Derived from the [personas](./personas.md) and ranked against the [baseline](./baseline.md). Each story names its persona, the pages it touches, a priority, and the evidence that proves it done. The canon column is the rule a design must not break while meeting the story. The [plan](./README.md) sequences them into waves.

Priorities: **P0** the discovery loop is broken without it · **P1** strong lift for the primary personas · **P2** polish or a secondary persona.

Pages: `/` front door · `S` `/search` · `T` `/tracks` · `Ar` `/artists` · `Al` `/albums` · `L` `/labels` · `F` `/fresh`.
