# Discovery UX: decisions

The operator's rulings from the design interview on 2026-09-24, in the order the design tree was walked. Each row is binding on the programme in [README.md](./README.md) until the operator reopens it; where a ruling changes canon (PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md, VOICE.md), the canon edit ships in the same PR as the behaviour.

## The player (the Discman)

"Discman" is an internal codename only. It is a third-party trademark and never appears in public copy.

| #   | Question                                         | Ruling                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Q1  | A persistent preview player across public pages? | **Yes.** It lives in the public chrome and survives navigation; it replaces the orphaned-audio behaviour. DESIGN.md gains its component entry.                                                                                                                                              |
| Q2  | Visible when nothing is playing?                 | **Hidden until the first play**, then docked for the rest of the visit (paused or playing) until dismissed.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Q3  | Where it docks                                   | **A full-width bottom bar** on every size. It must still read as Fluncle, not a streaming clone: warm-dark pane, the One Sun budget, Oxanium position readout, no shadows. `/mix` hides it and keeps its own bar; the chromeless surfaces (`/radio`, `/galaxy`, `/pipeline`) never show it. |
| Q4  | What plays after one preview ends                | **The list becomes the queue.** Play on any row queues the list around it from that row and advances automatically; the bar shows `n/N`.                                                                                                                                                    |
| Q5  | End of the queue                                 | **Stop, and offer one way on:** "keep going" continues into the last track's sonic neighbours, or the next page on a paginated hub. Nothing plays that the listener didn't choose.                                                                                                          |
| —   | One sound at a time                              | Settled from the code: starting `/radio`, Stories, or a log page's video pauses the player.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| —   | Lock screen                                      | Media Session metadata and controls, so a phone in a pocket keeps its transport.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

## The row

| #   | Question                  | Ruling                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Q6  | Where play lives on a row | **The cover is the play button** (glyph on hover/focus; always visible, small, on touch); the rest of the row opens the track.                                                                    |
| —   | Rows with no preview      | Settled from the code: `/api/preview` falls back to Deezer by ISRC, then iTunes, so play shows when a row has a stored preview URL or an ISRC. The queue skips a miss, and that row's glyph dims. |
| Q7  | The date column           | **Removed.** The year sits on the metadata line; where the day matters, dates become group headers.                                                                                               |
| Q8  | Future-dated releases     | **Hidden until release day** from newest-first `/tracks`, `/fresh` and the front door; artist and label pages list them under "Upcoming".                                                         |
| Q9  | Covers on unlit rows      | **The real cover, unlit:** desaturated and dimmed, no gold frame or heat. Findings keep the full-colour lit frame.                                                                                |
| Q10 | Secondary actions         | **On the player for the playing track, plus a quiet ⋮ menu per row** (Spotify, more like this, share, save). The trailing Spotify mark moves into the menu.                                       |
| Q11 | Save                      | **Local-first for any track**, synced on sign-in. It needs a server change so saves accept catalogue tracks.                                                                                      |
| Q27 | Fluncle's note on lists   | **Stays on the log page only.** Lists add no note line and no extra reordering; findings are distinguished by light alone.                                                                        |

## Finding music by feel

| #         | Question                         | Ruling                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Q12, Q15a | How style words resolve          | **Data-seeded style engine.** Seeds are embedded tracks whose album's `discogs_styles` names exactly one DnB substyle; each style gets an outlier-trimmed MuQ centroid; catalogue tracks are assigned to the nearest style above a confidence margin, nightly, and only changed assignments are written (the Turso write quota). Beatport data is excluded by its terms (see the `beatport_url` comment in `apps/web/src/db/schema.ts`). MusicBrainz genres are a gap-filler only if Discogs proves too thin. |
| Q13, Q15a | Fallback and override            | A typed style lexicon in code (names, aliases, optional anchor artists), guarded by a test that every anchor resolves to an artist with a centroid. Styles the data can't seed fall back to anchor artists.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Q15a      | The gate                         | **A read-only measurement spike in Wave 0.** A style ships only with enough seeds (starting bar ≥150) and held-out accuracy of at least ~75%.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Q32       | Spike data access                | One read-only pull from production through the already-authenticated `turso` CLI; the analysis runs locally; the script and a results summary are committed, and no data is.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Q14       | The feel UI                      | **A scrolling style chip row** above the `/tracks` list, in `/search`'s zero state, and on the front door's search band; a chip sets `?sound=<style>` and combines with the other filters.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Q15       | Galaxies                         | **Removed from `/tracks`.** Styles are the catalogue's feel vocabulary; galaxies stay the lore map of findings, and each gains a plain one-line sound description wherever it appears.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Q16       | Labelling a track with its style | **Printed only when confident** (high margin, or a direct Discogs seed): a chip after the readout, linking to `/tracks?sound=`. Below that bar a style ranks a track but never labels it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Q17       | Moods and energy                 | **Out of this programme.** Mood words fall through to the existing tiers honestly; moods become a Wave 7 experiment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Q28       | `/search` type-ahead             | **Live as you type** for the deterministic tiers (debounced, `?q=` via replaceState); sentence queries that need the model tier wait for Enter.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Q29       | More like this                   | **Opens `/search`'s sonic view** for that track; its results play as a list, and the player keeps a trail of seed covers to step back through. A track without an embedding falls back to its lead artist's centroid; with neither, the action is hidden.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

## `/fresh` and the hubs

| #        | Question            | Ruling                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------- | ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Q18      | `/fresh` shape      | **Week buckets of releases** over the 30-day window. A release is the unit (its tracks fold out and play as a list); the marquee becomes the week's standouts, one per release; a browser-local "new since your last visit" line; an explicit end of the window.     |
| Q19      | Hub default order   | **Most tracks** (the existing `renderable_track_count`), under a "this month" strip fed by `/fresh`'s data. A URL switch offers Most tracks · Recently active · A–Z; "Recently active" ships with a new latest-release column, maintained incrementally and indexed. |
| Q20      | What a tile plays   | Album: its tracklist. Artist and label: **findings first, then newest**, up to 20 previewable tracks.                                                                                                                                                                |
| Q21, Q22 | Moving between hubs | **A Browse menu in the top bar** on every public page, front door included: Tracks, Artists, Albums, Labels, Fresh, with a style-chip shortcut row. Archive only; the lore pages stay in the footer and on the front door. A sheet on mobile.                        |

## Keep, share, follow

| #   | Question       | Ruling                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Q23 | Follow         | **Email alerts.** Follow an artist or label and get new releases by email.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Q24 | Cadence        | **One weekly "your follows" digest** on Friday; a week with nothing new sends nothing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Q25 | Identity       | **The email creates an account through a magic link** (Better Auth's `magicLink` plugin, which creates the user on verify). The follow is applied when the link lands. Newsletter on by default, through the existing `autoSubscribeAtSignup` hook. PRODUCT.md records that Follow needs an email because it delivers mail; nothing else gains a gate. |
| Q33 | Sign-in        | **Magic link becomes the primary way to join and sign in**; password sign-in stays for existing accounts; the mobile app is untouched.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Q26 | Digest sending | **Fully automatic from the first Friday**: a rave-02 `--no-agent` host timer, template-only (no model call), idempotent per subscriber per week, with a kill-switch setting and the run ledger.                                                                                                                                                        |
| —   | Share          | The native share sheet on mobile, copy-link on desktop; the target is the track's canonical URL.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
