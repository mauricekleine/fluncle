# Database scale release manifest

This manifest packages the database-scale release candidate for attended rollout from `program/turso-scale`. It is evidence and ordering metadata only: it executes no migration, deployment, flag change, backfill, hosted replay, or production action.

The exact release-candidate commit is `ebfc6bc3ca94e1c79c4b701f0233862cb275f6df`. This manifest is packaging metadata; its history is not a rollout leg and does not alter application or schema state.

## Range law

Each `base..tip` range below is base-exclusive and tip-inclusive: the exact set returned by `git rev-list --reverse <base>..<tip>`. The ranges are pairwise disjoint. Their explicit commit lists are authoritative and prevent a merge-topology change from silently widening a leg. Integration-only merge commits are recorded separately and have no rollout action of their own.

## Compatibility window

Migrations `0161` through `0168` are expansion legs; `0169`, `0170`, and `0171` are schema contractions in that exact order. They remain ordered in the generated Drizzle journal. The due-work, crawl, public-projection, receipt, Sonar, and admission default-off controls and their source-query fallbacks remain in the candidate. H2 removes only the superseded keyed receipt inspection route, subject to its exact caller floor. H4 removes six proven redundant or unreachable indexes; H8 removes the separately proven capture-priority singleton.

The attended release must stop on a red current or 2× budget, a failed equivalence or convergence invariant, an unclassified registry or index consumer, a failed package gate, a failed hosted scratch replay, or an unmet caller/flag prerequisite. This manifest never authorizes targeting production, opening a flag, running a backfill, applying a migration, or deploying a commit.
